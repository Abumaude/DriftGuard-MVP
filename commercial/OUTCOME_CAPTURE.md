# Post-Purchase Outcome, Testimonial and Referral Standard

Trust cannot stop at checkout.

## 1. Outcome capture

Ask at an appropriate point after the user has had a fair opportunity to obtain value.

Capture separately:

- intended outcome;
- actual outcome;
- time/effort invested;
- friction encountered;
- unexpected benefits;
- unresolved problems;
- whether the product matched the pre-purchase representation.

Do not convert satisfaction into a testimonial automatically.

## 2. Testimonial consent

A testimonial requires explicit permission.

Record:
- exact words approved for publication;
- name/role/company disclosure level approved;
- channels approved;
- whether editing is allowed;
- expiry/review date if appropriate.

Do not:
- turn private support messages into public testimonials without permission;
- remove qualifying language that materially changes meaning;
- imply typicality where the result is unusual;
- offer undisclosed compensation for praise.

## 3. Case studies

Separate:
- observed facts;
- customer statements;
- your interpretation;
- calculated results.

State relevant context and important dependencies.

## 4. Referral mechanism

Referrals should be easy but not pressured.

Acceptable:
- simple share link;
- optional referral credit;
- invitation to recommend to someone for whom the product is genuinely suitable.

Avoid:
- guilt;
- social pressure;
- blocking value behind referrals;
- incentives that encourage indiscriminate promotion without disclosure.

## 5. Recovery loop

Negative outcomes are commercially valuable evidence.

When a customer struggles:
1. identify whether the failure was fit, expectation, onboarding, product, support or external dependency;
2. resolve what can reasonably be resolved;
3. update the product profile or Honest Box if the issue reveals a recurring material limitation;
4. update the Evidence Ledger if a public claim no longer survives the evidence.

## 6. Reputation deposit rule

A transaction is not considered commercially complete when payment clears.

It closes when one of these states is known:
- useful outcome;
- informed non-use;
- fair refund/cancellation;
- unresolved failure logged for correction.

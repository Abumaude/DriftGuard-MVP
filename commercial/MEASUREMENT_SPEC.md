# Commercial Trust Measurement Spec

Purpose: observe commercial health without turning trust into a gameable vanity score.

## Measurement rule

Measures support judgement. They do not override the constitution.

The system deliberately avoids a single "Trust Score".

## Four views

### 1. Attention
Observe:
- qualified reach;
- direct traffic;
- branded search where available;
- repository/profile discovery;
- relevant audience growth.

Interpretation: Are more appropriate people finding us?

### 2. Trust
Observe:
- repeat voluntary visits/engagement;
- direct replies and substantive questions;
- unsolicited mentions;
- saved/bookmarked/shared resources;
- repeat use of free tools;
- stated source of discovery.

Interpretation: Are people choosing to return before being pushed?

### 3. Commercial
Observe:
- purchases;
- revenue;
- refund/cancellation reasons;
- repeat purchases;
- time to first value;
- support burden;
- qualified conversion, where qualification is explicit.

Interpretation: Does trust translate into sustainable exchange?

### 4. Reputation
Observe:
- referrals;
- organic recommendations;
- citations/links;
- GitHub stars/forks/contributors when relevant;
- invitations/partnerships;
- testimonials with consent.

Interpretation: Are other people carrying the distribution?

## Directional, not target

Do not use "trust-originated revenue ratio" as a target.

Attribution is too ambiguous and becomes gameable.

Instead, capture a simple first-party field where appropriate:

**How did you first hear about this?**

Suggested values:
- search;
- social post;
- recommendation/referral;
- GitHub/free tool;
- webinar/course;
- email;
- community;
- paid ad;
- other;
- prefer not to say.

Treat this as directional evidence, not ground truth.

## Quarterly review

For each live product, answer:

1. Which trust-generating assets produced repeat attention?
2. Which claims generated confusion or support burden?
3. Which disclosures prevented poor-fit purchases?
4. Which customer failures should change the Honest Box?
5. Which free assets generated organic discovery or recommendation?
6. Which paid customers returned or referred others?
7. Which metrics are becoming performative or gameable?
8. What should be added to the constitution, evidence ledger or product profile?

## Dashboard gate

Do not build a dashboard until:
- at least one product is live;
- transactions or meaningful free-use events exist;
- the fields have been captured consistently;
- a manual review has already proved useful.

Then render from the underlying ledger. Do not make the dashboard the system of record.

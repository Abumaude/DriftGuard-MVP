# Honest Box / Before You Pay

A reusable disclosure surface for product pages, READMEs, checkout pages, course pages and proposals.

The goal is not "radical honesty" theatre. The goal is to surface information that could materially change a sensible buyer's decision.

## Standard version

### Before you pay

**This is for you if**
- [real buyer condition]
- [real problem/state]
- [required capability/commitment]

**Do not buy this if**
- [genuine buyer type who is unlikely to benefit]
- [condition under which a cheaper/free alternative is sufficient]
- [requirement the buyer cannot or will not meet]

**What this does**
- [specific capability]
- [specific outcome it supports]

**What this does not do**
- [material limitation]
- [edge case / unsupported use]
- [important dependency]

**What you will still need to do**
- [manual effort]
- [input/data/content required]
- [verification or judgement required]

**A cheaper or free alternative may be enough if**
- [alternative]
- [circumstance]

**Known limitations**
- [limitation that a fair competitor would reasonably mention]
- [important uncertainty]

**Evidence behind the main claims**
- [claim] → [evidence-ledger claim ID]
- [claim] → [evidence-ledger claim ID]

**Refund / cancellation**
- [plain-language rule]

## Anti-theatre test

Before publishing, ask:

1. Are these limitations actually capable of costing us a sale?
2. Did we disclose the limitations a competent competitor would attack?
3. Have we hidden a structural weakness while highlighting harmless flaws?
4. Is "not for you" genuinely protective of the buyer?
5. Is any free alternative recommendation sincere?
6. Would we still publish this box if conversion fell slightly?
7. Does the delivery/onboarding experience honour the same disclosures?

If the answer to 1, 2, 4 or 7 is no, the box is not ready.
